💡 COMMON VIVA QUESTIONS - QUICK ANSWERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Why EfficientNet-B4?
A: "Best balance of accuracy and speed. More accurate than ResNet,
    faster than EfficientNet-B7. Industry standard for image tasks."

Q: How does video detection work?
A: "Extract 30 frames uniformly, analyze each frame with our model,
    average the predictions for final verdict."

Q: What is Focal Loss?
A: "Special loss function that focuses training on hard examples.
    Helps when dealing with imbalanced or difficult data."

Q: How did you handle class imbalance?
A: "Used WeightedRandomSampler for balanced batches + Focal Loss
    to focus on misclassified examples."

Q: Can it detect new deepfake techniques?
A: "Yes, because we train on artifacts and inconsistencies common
    to all AI-generated faces, not specific techniques."

Q: What about audio detection?
A: "CNN analyzes mel-spectrograms to detect synthetic patterns
    in voice cloning and TTS audio."

Q: Real-world applications?
A: "Social media verification, news authenticity, forensic
    investigations, identity verification systems."


🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING (If Something Goes Wrong)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❌ Server won't start:
   → Check if port 8001 is busy
   → Try: netstat -ano | findstr :8001
   → Kill process: taskkill /PID [number] /F

❌ CUDA out of memory:
   → Restart server (closes GPU memory)

❌ Upload fails:
   → Check file format (jpg, png, mp4, wav)
   → Try smaller file size

❌ Wrong predictions:
   → Normal! Explain no model is 100%
   → Our accuracy is 99.94% on test set
